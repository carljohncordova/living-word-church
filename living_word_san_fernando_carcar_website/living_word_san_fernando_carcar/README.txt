LIVING WORD SAN FERNANDO & CARCAR — WEBSITE STARTER

Included pages:
- index.html — Home
- about.html — About
- events.html — Events
- blog.html — Blog with front-end search
- contact.html — Contact form demo and visit information
- assets/style.css — responsive styling
- assets/script.js — mobile navigation, current year, blog search, contact form demo

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or open the folder in Cursor / VS Code.
3. For local development, you can use the Live Server extension.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
- The church history, schedules, events, contact details, and article content are placeholders/examples.
- Confirm exact campus addresses, service times, phone numbers, official email, and social pages.
- The contact form is a front-end demo and does not send messages. Connect it to a verified email via a secure backend, Formspree, Resend, or another form service. Never put private API keys in browser JavaScript.
- Images are loaded from Unsplash URLs and Google Fonts from Google Fonts, so internet access is needed for those assets. Replace with church-approved photos where possible.
- Add the official church logo in the header/footer if supplied; the current circular “LW” monogram is a temporary text mark.
- Check photo usage rights and obtain consent for photos of church members, especially children.
- Update the sample blog posts and event listings before the website goes live.

DESIGN DIRECTION
Warm, modern, welcoming church identity: deep teal/navy, muted gold, warm cream, elegant serif headings, clean readable sans-serif body text, strong calls to action, responsive mobile navigation, and clear routes for visitors to learn, connect, and attend.
